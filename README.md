## 👤 About me
Hi! I'm Pablo, a **Full Stack Developer** and Computer Engineer from Córdoba, Spain 📍

I have **3 years of experience** building and modernizing a logistics SaaS in active production. On the frontend I work with **React and TypeScript**; on the backend, APIs and microservices with **Symfony, Laravel and PHP**. I care about code quality, regression-safe refactoring and shipping reliable software, and I use AI-assisted development (Claude Code, agent skills, hooks) every day. 👾

<br>

## 💼 What I'm doing

-> **Full Stack Developer** at [Eurotransportcar](https://www.eurotransportcar.com/es/) (Oct 2023 – present): B2B integrations with automotive companies, REST APIs and microservices, and the legacy modernization of the platform (Symfony 2.8 → 6.4, PHP 7.1 → 8.1)

-> Graduated in **Computer Engineering** (Software Engineering major) at the [University of Córdoba](http://www.uco.es/)
